# Role-Specific Metric Discovery (reference)

Sales, Marketing, Customer Service, Operations, Project Management, and HR/Admin
metric discovery lists moved from the main resume-quantifier SKILL.md. Ask-first
and confidentiality rules still apply to every number found this way.

## Sales
- Quota attainment %, revenue generated, deals closed, average deal size,
  pipeline generated, new accounts, retention rate

## Marketing
- Leads generated, campaign ROI, engagement rates, follower growth,
  traffic lift, conversion rates

## Customer Service
- Tickets resolved per day, CSAT, average response time, first-contact
  resolution rate, NPS contribution

## Operations
- Efficiency gains, cost reductions, cycle times, error rates, throughput

## Project Management
- Projects delivered, budget sizes, team sizes, on-time rate, stakeholders managed

## HR/Admin
- Hires closed, time-to-fill, employee satisfaction, training completion,
  onboarding efficiency
