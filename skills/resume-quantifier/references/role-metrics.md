# Role-Specific Metric Discovery (reference)

Sales, Marketing, Customer Service, Operations, Project Management, and HR/Admin
metric discovery lists moved from the main resume-quantifier SKILL.md. Ask-first
and confidentiality rules still apply to every number found this way. For each
category, 2-3 discovery questions to ask the user — never fill the metric yourself.

## Sales
- Quota attainment %, revenue generated, deals closed, average deal size,
  pipeline generated, new accounts, retention rate
- Ask: "What was your quota and where did you land against it?"
- Ask: "What was your average deal size, and how many deals did you close per year?"

## Marketing
- Leads generated, campaign ROI, engagement rates, follower growth,
  traffic lift, conversion rates
- Ask: "Which campaign moved which number, and by how much?"
- Ask: "What did attribution reporting actually credit to your work?"

## Customer Service
- Tickets resolved per day, CSAT, average response time, first-contact
  resolution rate, NPS contribution
- Ask: "What did CSAT or NPS look like before vs. after your changes?"
- Ask: "How long did resolution take before vs. after?"

## Operations
- Efficiency gains, cost reductions, cycle times, error rates, throughput
- Ask: "What was the cycle time or error rate before vs. after?"
- Ask: "What did the process cost to run, in time or dollars?"

## Project Management
- Projects delivered, budget sizes, team sizes, on-time rate, stakeholders managed
- Ask: "How many projects ran simultaneously, at what budgets?"
- Ask: "What was the on-time / on-budget rate across your portfolio?"

## HR/Admin
- Hires closed, time-to-fill, employee satisfaction, training completion,
  onboarding efficiency
- Ask: "How many roles did you close per quarter, and how did time-to-fill change?"
- Ask: "What did onboarding look like before vs. after you changed it?"
