# Discipline Conventions & Role-Specific Emphasis

Moved from the main SKILL.md to keep it under ~250 lines.

## Role-Specific Emphasis

### Tenure-Track Faculty
Emphasize: (1) recent high-impact publications, (2) grants — especially independent funding, (3) research trajectory and vision, (4) teaching, (5) mentoring record.

### Postdoctoral Position
Emphasize: (1) publications from PhD and postdoc, (2) research experience and skills, (3) collaborations, (4) future research potential, (5) fellowships/any funding.

### Lecturer/Teaching Faculty
Emphasize: (1) teaching experience and evaluations, (2) course development, (3) pedagogical training, (4) undergraduate mentoring, (5) teaching awards.

### Research Scientist
Emphasize: (1) publications, (2) technical skills, (3) grant-writing experience, (4) collaboration record, (5) relevant research experience.

## Discipline-Specific Conventions

*These are common tendencies, not rules — verify the target field's and department's conventions before applying any of them.*

### Sciences (Biology, Chemistry, Physics)
- Author order matters: first author = did the work; last author = senior/PI
- Impact factors sometimes listed when the posting or field expects them
- Numbered publication lists common; conference presentations weighted less than journal articles
- Chronological or reverse-chronological publication order varies by subfield — match the target department

### Humanities (History, Literature, Philosophy)
- Single-author publications highly valued
- Books (especially monographs with prestigious presses) crucial
- Conference presentations important; "under review" listings acceptable
- Public scholarship and digital humanities increasingly valued
- CVs tend longer; dissertation and teaching detail weighted heavily

### Social Sciences
- Both solo and collaborative work valued
- Mix of journal articles and books; book contracts count
- Funded research important; policy impact/applied work can count
- Co-authorship norms vary (economics: alphabetical; psychology: contribution order)

### Engineering & Computing
- Top conference publications often outweigh journals (CS especially)
- Verify each venue's prestige rather than assuming journals > conferences

### Listing "In Preparation"
Never inflate: "in preparation" items are acceptable on academic CVs but must be real works actually being written — apply the truthfulness guardrail. Don't let the agent generate publication placeholders the user hasn't confirmed.
