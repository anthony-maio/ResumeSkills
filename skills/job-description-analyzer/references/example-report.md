## Strengths to Emphasize (top 3, with evidence)

1. **Payments-scale distributed systems** — ingestion ownership at user-confirmed scale maps directly to their billing-infrastructure emphasis.
2. **Incident leadership** — they list on-call as "load-bearing"; the postmortem-program story is the differentiator.
3. **Monolith decomposition at org level** — 4 teams aligned on a staged migration; evidence for their "own technical direction" requirement.

## Gaps — questions to ask BEFORE addressing them

- **Kubernetes operators (missing):** "Have you written or operated custom Kubernetes operators or controllers? To what depth?" Do not write any claim about this until answered.
- **ML/LLM platform (partial):** "What exactly did you ship on the RAG prototype — retrieval, eval, serving? What was production vs. demo?" Keep the eventual claim at exactly that depth.

No courses, certifications, or skill additions go into any artifact without the user's confirmation.
